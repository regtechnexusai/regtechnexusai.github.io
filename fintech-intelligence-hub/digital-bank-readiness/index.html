Risk signals

Critical-gate review

Critical-gate status takes precedence over maturity reporting. A low score alone is not a confirmed legal or supervisory failure; “no critical gate identified” does not establish compliance. A critical-gate N/A is an exception requiring escalation and withholds the aggregate score. When a critical failure is confirmed, the calculated score remains visible for diagnostic transparency only; the readiness interpretation is overridden.

Portable and protected

Encrypted export / import

No assessment is automatically saved to browser storage. Export before leaving the page if you need to preserve your work.

Export uses PBKDF2-SHA-256 and AES-256-GCM through Web Crypto. Keep the password separate from the export file. A lost password cannot be recovered. Imported content is validated before it replaces the current in-memory assessment.

How to interpret results

  • Internal maturity bands are methodology labels, not official supervisory ratings.
  • Incomplete answers and unverified N/A exclusions prevent an aggregate score.
  • Evidence confidence is not the same as a maturity score.
  • Critical gate outcomes need documented professional review and jurisdiction-specific context.
  • International standards are not automatically domestic law; check legal status, applicability and effective date.

Method: DISCOVER → INSPECT → VERIFY → TEST → CHALLENGE → ASSESS → RISK-RATE → REMEDIATE → RETEST → ASSURE.
