Minimum instrument-level record

  • Issuer, jurisdiction, exact instrument title/number and official URL.
  • Publication/revision date, effective date, transition period and supersession status.
  • Instrument class: law, regulation, binding notice, standard, guidance, consultation or report.
  • Entity/product/activity scope and applicability assumptions.
  • Requirement-to-control mapping, affected tool/module, reviewer, review date and next review trigger.
  • Prior version, change summary, test evidence and affected calculation/output checks.

Severity and escalation

  • Critical: a superseded source materially invalidates a live output or instruction.
  • High: missing or ambiguous primary source affects a material banking, AML/CFT, sanctions or AI-governance conclusion.
  • Medium: metadata, applicability explanation or traceability is incomplete without a confirmed incorrect result.
  • Low: editorial or link-quality issue without material impact.

Where evidence is incomplete, record INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE — VERIFICATION REQUIRED. A working URL or search snippet alone does not establish current status.
