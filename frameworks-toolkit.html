The VERIFY → REVIEW → APPROVE model

Verify: establish the source, date, scope, evidence and limitations.

Review: assess the signal or requirement in context, challenge assumptions and record exceptions.

Approve: assign the accountable decision-maker, document the rationale and define follow-up.

This framework is a decision-support aid. It does not replace applicable law, regulation, internal policy or professional judgment.

Where to apply it

  • Regulatory change and control-impact review
  • Financial-crime and transaction-risk triage
  • Responsible AI use-case assessment
  • Supervisory and assurance workpaper design
