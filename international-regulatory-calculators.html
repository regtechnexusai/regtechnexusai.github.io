Regulatory calculation governance

Formula structure is separated from jurisdictional parameters. Basel provides global calculation standards, while national implementation can add different definitions, buffers, scope, transitional arrangements and reporting rules. Bangladesh Bank's Stress Testing 2024 guidance requires banks to maintain a rigorous, forward-looking stress-testing framework integrated with risk management and capital adequacy processes.

The Basel Framework currently contains dedicated standards for risk-based capital, credit/market/operational RWA, LCR, NSFR and leverage; its framework page records changes effective in 2026 and future changes in 2027.
