Regulatory calculation governance

Regulatory-status labels distinguish verified benchmark references from analytical approximations. Formula structure is separated from jurisdictional parameters. Basel provides global calculation standards, while national implementation can add different definitions, buffers, scope, transitional arrangements and reporting rules. Bangladesh Bank's Stress Testing 2024 guidance requires banks to maintain a rigorous, forward-looking stress-testing framework integrated with risk management and capital adequacy processes.

Version control: This public page is reviewed against cited primary sources; jurisdictional rules, supervisory interpretations and transitional arrangements can change. Verify the applicable rule at the assessment date.

The Basel Framework currently contains dedicated standards for risk-based capital, credit/market/operational RWA, LCR, NSFR and leverage. The framework is versioned: some standards have 2026 changes and operational-risk OPE25 has a further version scheduled for 1 January 2027.

Regulatory-use boundary

Important: These calculators are independent decision-support and educational tools. A verified benchmark confirms only the cited threshold or formula; it does not mean the calculator reproduces the complete supervisory methodology, reporting template, prudential buffer stack, transitional arrangement or institution-specific requirement. The 10 added national modules include core analytical capital, liquidity, leverage, consumer-finance and third-party dependency indicators; jurisdiction-specific thresholds, definitions and supervisory reporting components remain validation dependent.
