OUR PUBLIC-BENEFIT COMMITMENT

Free agricultural knowledge comes first.

MUKUL is designed to earn trust through practical value—not by putting essential knowledge behind a paywall. The approach is global, multilingual and grounded in local realities.

Stage 1 · Free for everyone

The first priority is accessible agricultural learning and useful tools for growers, students, small businesses and communities, regardless of country or income.

  • Core educational content and practical guides at no charge
  • Mobile-friendly access and clear language choices
  • Reliable references, publication dates and visible limitations
  • No claims that prototype features are already live

Stage 2 · Sustainable services

Only after genuine user needs are understood may optional business services be explored. Any future revenue model should support the mission rather than undermine access.

  • Optional trade connections and business tools
  • Potential institutional partnerships or sponsorships with transparency
  • Keep essential public learning resources free wherever feasible
  • No unverified listings, hidden commissions or misleading offers
MUKUL-এর পণ্য-নীতিমালা সম্পর্কে

MUKUL will use its own brand, interface, writing, code and product decisions. General problem areas may inform research, but third-party code, proprietary content, branding, datasets or distinctive designs will not be copied.

AGRICULTURAL KNOWLEDGE

Practical guidance, grounded in sources.

The intended knowledge layer will prioritise traceable references, local adaptation, publication dates and clear limits on automated advice.

For growers and advisers

A structured route to crop-specific learning and farm planning support.

  • Crop growth and management fundamentals
  • Pest and disease identification education
  • Weather, water and soil context
  • Source links and clear uncertainty statements

For buyers and agribusinesses

Practical information to improve product enquiries and supplier conversations.

  • Product specification and quality terminology
  • Post-harvest handling and storage basics
  • Market and trade-readiness checklists
  • Traceability, evidence and recordkeeping principles
Responsible-use boundary: MUKUL's planned educational tools are not a substitute for qualified agronomists, local agricultural authorities, laboratory testing or applicable product labels. The platform must not invent pesticide or fertiliser dosages, diagnose from insufficient evidence, or present unverified market information as fact.
