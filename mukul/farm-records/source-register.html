MUKUL — Source verification register

Review date: 18 October 2026. This is an initial source register, not a claim that every source has been reviewed for every crop, location or reuse permission.

  1. Bangladesh Agricultural Research Council (BARC) — Official website. Verify publication date, applicability and reuse terms for each document.
  2. BARC Fertilizer Recommendation Guide 2024 — Official PDF. Do not use this prototype to generate fertilizer rates.
  3. BARC Agri-Advisory / Crop Zoning Portal — Official portal. Location and crop applicability must be checked directly.
  4. Bangladesh Rice Research Institute (BRRI) — Cultivation guidance. Verify current recommendations for the specific rice season and variety.
  5. Bangladesh Agricultural Research Institute (BARI) — Official website. Select crop-specific publications only after review.

Evidence and limitations

Keep source title, issuing body, version/date, applicable crop and region, review date, reviewer, and reuse permission with every future recommendation. If evidence is insufficient, state “INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE — VERIFICATION REQUIRED.” Listing a link does not grant permission to copy or translate it.

This tool does not diagnose crop disease and does not estimate fertilizer/pesticide dosages.
