TECHNICAL AND OPERATIONAL BOUNDARY

Transparent limits are part of the product.

01

Browser-local public build

Published calculator and toolkit inputs are processed in the browser. The public build does not present a backend case database, institutional login, RBAC, server-side audit trail or bank-system integration.

02

No live screening claim

The public tools do not claim live sanctions, PEP, adverse-media, SWIFT, core-banking or market-price feeds. A displayed score is not a verified external fact.

03

Inspectable rules

Browser-side logic can be inspected or altered by a user. This is acceptable for a public educational build, but not for an institutional decision-influencing workflow without server-side execution and change control.

04

Evidence before reliance

Every bank use case requires source hierarchy, version pinning, independent recomputation, evidence retention, authorised review and a documented decision owner.

CONDITIONS PRECEDENT

What should happen before a bank relies on an output.

  1. Use synthetic data first. Do not enter customer names, account data, transaction details, confidential documents or MNPI into the public build.
  2. Pin the exact build. Record the product, version, date, source assumptions and change-log entry used for every exercise or pilot.
  3. Recompute independently. Test boundary values, missing inputs, conflicting indicators, critical overrides and rounding behaviour against an approved reference.
  4. Map the output. Link every result to the bank’s applicable law, circular, policy, control objective, owner, evidence and escalation route.
  5. Escalate institutional use. Any use beyond training or sandbox discussion requires an information-security review, privacy review, vendor due diligence, access control, auditability, retention and incident-response design.
  6. Keep the human decision. The platform must not be the sole basis for customer approval, transaction rejection, account action, STR decision, regulatory submission or Board appraisal conclusion.
