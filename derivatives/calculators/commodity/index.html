01 · FUTURES, FORWARDS & BASIS

Commodity price and contract economics

MODEL GOVERNANCE

Validation boundary

These calculators are client-side educational and analytical models. Inputs are processed in the browser and are not a substitute for exchange, CCP, bank or institutional valuation engines.

Model status: illustrative formulas · internal source review · regression validation recommended before institutional reliance.

Known limitations: contract conventions, exercise style, curves, volatility surfaces, settlement rules, collateral terms, netting, liquidity, location/quality basis and CCP margin methodologies may materially change production results.

Verification rule: independently recompute material outputs and retain inputs, units, model/version, expected result, actual result and tolerance. Where evidence is insufficient: INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE — VERIFICATION REQUIRED.

Commodity Futures Contract Value & P&L

Calculates contract notional and price-movement P&L using a stated unit quantity and number of contracts.

Sample: 5 contracts × 1,000 units; entry 75; exit 78; Long.

Commodity Forward / Futures Carry

Indicative cost-of-carry relationship with storage/insurance costs and convenience yield, using continuous compounding and Actual/365 maturity conversion.

Sample: spot 75; financing 5%; storage/insurance 2%; convenience yield 1%; 180 days.

Commodity Basis

Measures the commodity basis as Cash/Spot − Futures and expresses the basis relative to spot.

Sample: cash 74; futures 76; 90 days.

Commodity Contract Unit Conversion

Converts a quoted commodity price into contract value using unit quantity and an optional conversion factor.

Sample: $2.50 × 42,000 units × 2 contracts. Change the conversion factor when units differ.
02 · COMMODITY OPTIONS

Options on futures — Black-76 style analysis

Commodity Futures Option — Black-76 Price

Indicative European-style option on a futures price. Black-76 assumes positive forward/futures and strike inputs; it is not a universal model for American-style commodity options or negative-price environments.

Model boundary: Black-76 · continuous compounding · Actual/365 · positive F and K · educational/analytical use.

Sample: F=75, K=78, vol=30%, rate=5%, 90 days, Call. F, K, volatility and maturity must be > 0.

Commodity Option Greeks

Indicative Black-76 Delta, Gamma, Vega and Theta for a European-style futures option, with contract scaling.

Model boundary: Black-76 Greeks · Actual/365 · Theta per calendar day. American exercise, early-exercise effects and negative-price models are outside scope.

Sample: ATM; 30% volatility; 90 days; 10 contracts × 1,000 multiplier. F, K, volatility and maturity must be > 0.
03 · COMMODITY SPREADS

Calendar, intercommodity and processing spreads

Calendar Spread

Calculates the price differential between two futures contract months and the spread position value. This is not P&L unless an entry spread is also supplied.

Sample: near 75; deferred 73; 1,000 units; 3 long spreads. Output is spread position value, not entry-to-exit P&L.

Intercommodity Spread

Calculates the price relationship between two related commodities using a specified ratio.

Sample: A=80, B=72, ratio 1:1. Adjust ratios for the actual hedge structure.

Crack Spread

Illustrative refinery margin between refined product and crude oil. Converts cents/gallon to dollars/barrel using 42 gallons per barrel.

Sample: product $2.50/gal; crude $70/bbl; 42 gal/bbl. This is a simplified 1:1 illustration.

Crush Spread

Illustrative agricultural processing margin: product values minus feedstock cost, using user-specified yield and prices.

Sample values are illustrative; actual soybean/canola crush economics require contract-specific units, yields and conversions.
04 · HEDGING, PHYSICAL ECONOMICS & COLLATERAL

Commodity risk-management scenarios

Commodity Hedge Ratio

Minimum-variance hedge ratio using correlation and price volatility, then scales the result to the physical exposure.

Sample: correlation 0.85; physical vol 18%; futures vol 20%; exposure 10m. Correlation must be −1 to +1; volatility and exposure must be > 0.

Storage & Carry Cost

Estimates physical carry cost from storage, insurance, handling and financing over a stated period.

Sample: 10,000 units; storage $0.20 + handling/insurance $0.08 per unit/month; 6 months; value 750k.

Commodity Swap Settlement

Simplified fixed-for-floating commodity swap cash-flow calculation for a single period.

Sample: 10,000 units; fixed 75; floating 78. Actual swaps depend on confirmation, averaging and settlement conventions.

Commodity Margin Stress Scenario

Simple scenario for estimating exposure after an adverse price shock and an indicative margin rate. Not a CCP margin model.

Sample: price 75; adverse shock 12%; 100,000 units; indicative margin rate 10%.
05 · CONTROL & MODEL BOUNDARY

Why commodity calculations need product-specific governance

Commodity derivatives can embed physical delivery, location basis, quality/grade differentials, seasonality, storage constraints, convenience yield, currency exposure and contract-specific settlement rules. Calendar spreads and crack spreads are distinct risk structures, not merely generic price differences. CME materials explicitly describe calendar spread options and crack spreads as specialised commodity risk-management structures. citeturn0search3turn0search1

For institutional use, retain contract specification, price source, unit conversion, market-data timestamp, valuation convention, model/version, assumptions, approvals and reconciliation evidence. Where a result depends on exchange or bilateral documentation, verify the applicable primary source before relying on it.
