01 · PRICING & P&L

Futures, forwards and options

Futures P&L

Illustrative profit/loss from price movement. Enter contract size and number of contracts.

Sample: Long 2 contracts × 1,000 units; entry 100; exit 105.

FX Forward Rate

Covered-interest-parity style indicative forward rate for an FX forward.

Sample: Spot 110; domestic 5%; foreign 3%; 180 days; 360-day basis.

European Option — Black-Scholes

Indicative European call/put value plus Delta, Gamma, Vega and Theta. Assumes continuous rates/dividend yield and lognormal dynamics.

Sample: S=100, K=105, volatility=20%, rate=5%, expiry=90 days, Call.

Option Payoff / Breakeven

Shows expiration payoff and the basic breakeven level for a long option.

Sample: Long Call, K=100, premium=5, expiry price=112, multiplier=100.
02 · RATES & SWAPS

Interest-rate calculations

Interest-Rate Swap Payment

Illustrative fixed and floating payment for one calculation period.

Sample: Notional 10,000,000; fixed 5%; floating 4.5%; quarter-year period.

FRA Settlement

Indicative cash settlement of a Forward Rate Agreement using a simple money-market convention.

Sample: Notional 10m; FRA 5%; reference 5.5%; accrual 0.25.

DV01 / PVBP

Simple linear approximation of value change for a 1 basis-point rate move.

Sample: PV 10m; modified duration 4.5; 1 bp move.

Indicative Swap Fixed Rate

Par fixed rate using a set of discount factors: fixed rate ≈ (1−DFn) / sum(DFᵢ×accrualᵢ). Enter comma-separated discount factors and equal accrual periods.

Sample discount factors: 0.987, 0.973, 0.958, 0.943; quarterly accrual.
03 · RISK, GREEKS & EXPOSURE

Risk sensitivities

Option Position Greeks

Uses the Black-Scholes result to scale Delta, Gamma and Vega by contracts and multiplier.

Sample: ATM option; 20% vol; 90 days; 10 contracts × 100 multiplier.

Forward / Futures Basis

Measures the difference between the observed futures/forward price and spot price.

Sample: Spot 100; forward 102; 90 days.
04 · HEDGING & MARGIN

Exposure, hedge and capital planning

Minimum-Variance Hedge Ratio

Indicative hedge ratio from correlation and volatility of exposure and hedge instrument.

Sample: correlation 0.85; exposure vol 12%; hedge vol 10%; exposure 10m.

Indicative Margin

Simple scenario-based margin estimate. This is not a CCP margin model such as SPAN/CORE or an approved regulatory IM model.

Sample: exposure 10m; indicative margin rate 8%; collateral 300k; haircut 5%.

Option Strategy Breakeven

Basic expiration breakeven for a single long call or put using premium paid.

Sample: K=100; premium=6; Long Call → breakeven 106.

Collateral Interest

Simple collateral interest accrual using principal, rate, day count and days. Market documentation determines the actual convention.

Sample: collateral 5m; rate 4%; 30 days; 360-day basis.
05 · COVERAGE & CONTROL

What this suite covers

The suite intentionally focuses on core calculation families commonly encountered in derivatives pricing, risk and collateral workflows. Professional platforms may additionally use yield-curve bootstrapping, OIS discounting, multi-curve valuation, volatility surfaces, local/stochastic volatility, Monte Carlo, CVA/DVA/FVA, XVA, expected exposure, PFE, stress testing, portfolio margining and product-specific clearing models. Those require materially more market data and model governance than a public educational calculator.

Model-governance principle: A calculator output is only as reliable as its inputs, conventions, model assumptions, market data and validation. For institutional use, retain calculation inputs, source data, model version, assumptions, approvals and evidence.
