Derivatives Readiness Check

Use this self-assessment to identify where a derivatives control environment may need stronger evidence, testing or management attention. It is a screening aid—not a regulatory determination.

How to use: For each area, select the answer that best reflects the current evidence position. “Not evidenced” should be treated as an evidence gap, not automatically as a control failure.

Interpretation boundary: The score is an internal screening indicator based only on the selections made here. It is not a regulatory compliance rating, audit opinion, legal conclusion or investment assessment. Where evidence is missing, use INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE — VERIFICATION REQUIRED.
