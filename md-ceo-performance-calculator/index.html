Measurement Calculator.

Explore a transparent 100-point calculation model aligned to Bangladesh Bank’s Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) Framework for Managing Director (MD)/Chief Executive Officer (CEO) of Banks.

BRPD-2 Circular No. 5 · 13 September 2026Read the official circular ↗
Independent calculation aid. The official assessment must use Board-approved targets, the bank’s immediate previous-quarter baseline, verified internal/regulatory evidence and Bangladesh Bank’s prescribed Excel template. Public web data alone cannot complete all 30 KPIs. This Phase 1 public build runs in the browser and has no backend, login, RBAC, case database or server-side audit trail. This is an independent baseline-to-target progress model, not a validated reproduction of the prescribed Excel scoring formula. The calculator implements the circular’s critical-KPI deduction: 25% of the maximum achievable weighted score for a listed critical KPI when its achievement is below 50% or receives zero. It remains a review-support aid—not an official Bangladesh Bank rating—and must be reconciled with the official template before reliance.

Process: Higher-is-better and lower-is-better KPIs use proportionate movement from baseline toward the Board target. Target/limit-aligned KPIs are scored by how close the actual is to the Board target relative to the baseline gap. Achievement below 50% receives zero for that KPI. The contribution then equals achievement × lens weight × KPI weight, subject to the circular’s critical-KPI deduction rule.

Critical-KPI example: Gross NPL has a maximum weighted contribution of 25% × 25% = 6.25 points. If its achievement is below 50%, its contribution is zero and the additional critical deduction is 25% × 6.25 = 1.5625 points. This arithmetic follows paragraph 2.5(ii); the achievement mapping itself still requires reconciliation with the official Excel template.

In the live calculator, the Board must approve the target, the bank must retain evidence, and all 30 KPI rows should be reconciled with the prescribed Excel template.

CONCEPT NOTE

RegTech Nexus AI MD/CEO Performance Measurement Calculator

Proposed as a transparent review-support layer for bank Boards, Board committees, governance professionals, researchers and supervisors. It is designed to organise evidence and make the arithmetic reviewable—not to replace the Board’s appraisal judgment or Bangladesh Bank’s regulatory review.

01

Purpose

Translate the 2026 BB KPI framework into a readable, auditable and reusable calculation interface for the five performance lenses.

02

Core method

Each KPI receives an illustrative baseline-to-target progress score; this mapping must be checked against the prescribed Excel template before any regulatory use. Higher, lower and target/limit-aligned directions are shown separately; categorical, count-based and composite parameters must first be mapped to an approved numeric scale. The five lens allocations are 25%, 25%, 10%, 25% and 15%; each lens is normalised to 100% before its weight is applied. Achievement below 50% receives zero for that KPI, and the listed critical-KPI deduction follows paragraph 2.5(ii) of the circular.

03

Public-data layer

Bank websites, annual reports, half-yearly reports, quarterly disclosures and BB publications can prefill selected observable metrics with a source and period label.

04

Evidence boundary

Board targets, RBS ratings, AML/CFT ratings, inspection observations, internal controls, regulatory reporting quality and legal-risk evidence are not reliably public and remain manual inputs.
