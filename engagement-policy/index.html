Institutional Engagement & Operational Governance Policy

RegTech Nexus AI uses documented digital channels for service enquiries, institutional evaluation, delivery coordination and support. The policy separates the organisation's official accountability from the personal availability of any individual founder.

Version 1.0Effective: November 2026Public policy

1. Operating model: digital by design

RegTech Nexus AI's public website is delivered through a static GitHub Pages architecture. Public pages and browser-based tools must not be interpreted as evidence of a server-side client portal, bank connectivity, enterprise identity management or a hosted confidential-data processing environment.

Institutional enquiries and agreed service engagements are handled through designated written channels. This supports traceable communication, consistent handling and documented review. Digital delivery does not remove the need for professional judgment, human oversight or institutional accountability.

3. Data-handling prerequisites

  • Do not upload or email unmasked personally identifiable information, account numbers, customer records, credentials, transaction-level banking data or supervisory-confidential information to public website tools.
  • Use synthetic, anonymised or appropriately redacted examples for public demonstrations.
  • Before any institutional data-processing engagement, the parties must agree the permitted data, purpose, processing environment, access controls, retention period, deletion process and applicable contractual safeguards.
  • No zero-retention, zero-knowledge, encryption-strength or security-certification claim is made unless the relevant control has been implemented, independently evidenced where appropriate and scoped to the specific service.

4. Office address and corporate identity

The address published on the website is provided for official correspondence and applicable administrative or statutory purposes. It does not provide walk-in client consultations, ad hoc sales meetings or on-site technical support.

Corporate identity, registration details and other legal particulars are represented only to the extent supported by current and applicable records. The presence of an address does not imply a staffed client reception facility.

5. Service accountability

Each engagement must define its scope, inputs, deliverables, review responsibility, applicable timeline, fees (if any), data-handling terms and escalation route in writing. Any service-level commitment applies only where expressly agreed for that engagement.

Core principle: No physical founder access is offered. Institutional accountability, documented commitments and appropriate official communication remain in place.
