# RegTech Nexus AI website update package

This package combines the main RegTech Nexus AI website update with the launch communications discussed today.

## Included

- `index.html` — refreshed homepage with an **Insights & Updates** section.
- `insights.html` — website-native archive page containing the supplied Facebook and LinkedIn launch posts, with links back to the website and official social pages.
- `styles.css` — exact uploaded logo asset, responsive mobile navigation styling and restrained navy / royal blue / teal / muted-gold ambient light effects for the white space.
- `regtech-nexus-ai-logo.jpg` — the supplied logo asset preserved as an image; it is used without recolouring or distortion.
- `script.js` — mobile menu behaviour.
- `privacy.html` and `terms.html` — supporting pages for the footer links.
- `robots.txt`, `sitemap.xml`, `seo-config.json` and `SEO-MANAGEMENT.md` — page metadata, structured data, crawl guidance and an editable SEO maintenance workflow.
- `AUDIT-REMEDIATION.md` and `CONTACT-SETUP.md` — addressed audit findings and documented the remaining live-host, security, performance, indexing, accessibility and domain-email checks.
- `blog.html`, `regulatory-explainers.html`, `frameworks-toolkit.html`, `publications.html`, `contact.html` and three article pages — real content destinations for the Knowledge and Insights navigation.
- `payscale-2026/` — live on-domain scenario calculator with editable phase assumptions and a non-official pay-fixation disclaimer.
