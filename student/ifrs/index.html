PROFESSIONAL APPLICATION LAB

From standard to defensible conclusion

A repeatable workflow for accounting, audit, banking, finance, reporting and governance teams. Use it to organise analysis; do not treat it as an automatic accounting conclusion.

01 · Frame the issue

Record entity, jurisdiction, reporting period, transaction facts, contract terms and the exact accounting question. Identify missing facts before choosing a treatment.

02 · Analyse recognition and measurement

Separate classification, recognition, measurement, presentation and disclosure. State the applicable criteria, assumptions, calculations and alternatives considered.

03 · Evidence and review

Link the conclusion to contracts, ledgers, reconciliations, model inputs, approvals and source versions. Record preparer, reviewer, unresolved exceptions and escalation points.

04 · Financial statement impact

Check statement line items, note disclosures, estimates, judgements, materiality, related parties and consistency across the financial statements.

05 · Banking and IFRS 9

Connect financial instrument classification and expected-credit-loss analysis to data quality, forward-looking assumptions, model governance and independent challenge.

06 · Distinguish IFRS from local rules

Keep accounting requirements distinct from prudential regulation, tax law, company law, local adoption decisions and regulator-specific reporting templates.

Selected Standards

IFRS 9

Financial instruments: classification and measurement, impairment and hedge accounting are major learning areas.

IFRS 15

Revenue from contracts with customers: study the five-step model and the importance of identifying performance obligations and transaction price.

IFRS 16

Leases: understand the lessee accounting model and why identifying a lease and measuring related balances matters.

IAS 2

Inventories: understand cost, measurement and the role of net realisable value.

IAS 7

Cash flow statements: distinguish operating, investing and financing cash flows and understand presentation principles.

IAS 1

Presentation of financial statements: study presentation and disclosure concepts while checking the latest applicable requirements.

GUIDED CASE WORKSHOP

Practise applying the standard

Try to identify the issue and evidence needed before opening the suggested approach. The scenarios are educational examples, not determinations for real transactions.

IFRS 9 · Borrower risk worsens

A borrower is not past due, but its revenue and cash-flow forecasts have deteriorated materially.

Reveal guided approach

Identify the instrument and impairment approach; assess changes in credit risk since initial recognition; examine forward-looking assumptions and relevant evidence; document model limitations, overlays and review. The facts provided are insufficient to determine a final stage or allowance.

IFRS 15 · Equipment plus support

A supplier sells equipment, installation and two years of support for one upfront contract price.

Reveal guided approach

Identify promised goods and services; assess whether they are distinct performance obligations; determine and allocate the transaction price; evaluate when each obligation is satisfied. Upfront payment alone does not decide when revenue is recognised.

IFRS 16 · Lease extension option

A company leases office space and has an extension option that management expects to use.

Reveal guided approach

Review the enforceable period, option terms and economic incentives; apply the relevant assessment threshold; document commencement measurement and any reassessment or modification issues. Informal intention alone is not enough.
