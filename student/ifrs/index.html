Financial Reporting Foundation

Purpose of Reporting

Financial reporting communicates information about an entity's financial position, financial performance and cash flows to users making economic decisions.

Conceptual Framework

Study the objectives, qualitative characteristics, elements, recognition and measurement concepts that support financial reporting.

Recognition & Measurement

Learn why accounting conclusions depend on the applicable standard, facts, definitions, recognition criteria and measurement basis.

Selected Standards

IFRS 9

Financial instruments: classification and measurement, impairment and hedge accounting are major learning areas.

IFRS 15

Revenue from contracts with customers: study the five-step model and the importance of identifying performance obligations and transaction price.

IFRS 16

Leases: understand the lessee accounting model and why identifying a lease and measuring related balances matters.

IAS 2

Inventories: understand cost, measurement and the role of net realisable value.

IAS 7

Cash flow statements: distinguish operating, investing and financing cash flows and understand presentation principles.

IAS 1

Presentation of financial statements: study presentation and disclosure concepts while checking the latest applicable requirements.

Quick Self-Test

1. Why should students verify the current version of an IFRS requirement?
Standards and amendments change. Use the current authoritative IFRS Foundation material for conclusions.
2. What is IFRS 15 primarily concerned with?
Revenue from contracts with customers and the principles for recognising revenue.
3. What is a key study point for IFRS 9?
Financial instruments, including classification and measurement, impairment and hedge accounting.
