📖 Theorem & Proof Library

Pythagorean Theorem

Statement: In a right triangle, a²+b²=c².

Proof sketch: Compare the area of a square of side a+b with four copies of the triangle; expanding both expressions yields the identity.

Fundamental Theorem of Arithmetic

Statement: Every integer greater than 1 has a unique prime factorisation, apart from ordering.

Proof sketch: Existence follows by repeated factorisation; uniqueness follows from Euclid's lemma.

Intermediate Value Theorem

Statement: A continuous function on [a,b] attains every value between f(a) and f(b).

Proof sketch: Use the supremum of the set where f is below the target and continuity at the boundary point.

Mean Value Theorem

Statement: If f is continuous on [a,b] and differentiable on (a,b), some c has f′(c)=(f(b)−f(a))/(b−a).

Proof sketch: Subtract the secant line and apply Rolle's theorem.

Bayes' Theorem

Statement: P(A|B)=P(B|A)P(A)/P(B), P(B)>0.

Proof sketch: Express P(A∩B) in two conditional forms and equate them.

Rank–Nullity Theorem

Statement: dim(V)=rank(T)+nullity(T) for a linear map on finite-dimensional V.

Proof sketch: Extend a basis of ker(T) to a basis of V; images of the added vectors form a basis of im(T).

💼 Professional & Applied Projects

Finance — Discounted Cash Flow

Build a cash-flow table, apply PV=FV/(1+r)^n, then test sensitivity to r and timing.

Risk — Expected Loss

For PD=2%, LGD=40%, EAD=500,000 calculate EL=PD×LGD×EAD and vary one input at a time.

AI/ML — Gradient Descent

For J(θ)=(θ−3)² use θₜ₊₁=θₜ−η∇J(θₜ); compare learning rates.

Engineering — Exponential Model

Use y(t)=y₀e^{kt}; estimate k from two observations and inspect residuals.

Cryptography — Modular Arithmetic

Compute congruences and modular inverses; explain why coprimality controls invertibility.

Optimisation — Linear Programming

Define an objective and resource constraints, then inspect feasible solutions and boundary cases.

🔬 Research Hub

Workflow: Question → Definitions → Literature map → Hypothesis/Conjecture → Method → Computation → Validation → Limitations → Reproducible record.
Open-question exercises: investigate prime-gap patterns; test sequence convergence; compare numerical methods for one differential equation; vary constraints in an optimisation model.
Reproducibility checklist: record inputs, formulae, parameters, units, software/version, tolerance, random seed where relevant, expected result and observed result.
Starting points: arXiv · AMS journals · SIAM publications.
