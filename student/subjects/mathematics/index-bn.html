আপনার স্তর অনুযায়ী গণিত শিখুন

গণিতের শেখা intuitive foundation থেকে rigorous proof, research এবং industry modelling পর্যন্ত বিস্তৃত। প্রতিটি স্তর আলাদা হলেও prerequisite ও knowledge connection বজায় থাকবে।

LEVEL 01 · FOUNDATION / SCHOOL

ভিত্তি ও ধারণা গড়ুন

Confidence, concepts and mathematical reasoning.

  • Arithmetic, algebra, geometry and basic statistics
  • Visual explanations, puzzles and real-life examples
  • Step-by-step “why and how” solutions
LEVEL 02 · SECONDARY

বিশ্লেষণী দক্ষতা গড়ুন

Move from formulas to functions, models and structured problem solving.

  • Trigonometry, calculus, vectors and probability
  • Formula revision and graph-based exploration
  • Exam-style and timed practice
LEVEL 03 · UNDERGRADUATE

গাণিতিকভাবে চিন্তা করুন

Develop definitions, theorems, proof and applied mathematics.

  • Analysis, linear algebra, algebra and discrete mathematics
  • Definitions → lemmas → theorems → proofs
  • Problem sets and mathematical writing
LEVEL 04 · GRADUATE / MASTER'S

অ্যাবস্ট্র্যাকশন গভীর করুন

Work with advanced structures, rigorous arguments and computation.

  • Measure, functional analysis, topology and advanced algebra
  • LaTeX, symbolic and numerical experimentation
  • Advanced references and proof-oriented exercises
LEVEL 05 · RESEARCH / PHD

গবেষণামূলক প্রশ্ন অনুসন্ধান করুন

Use mathematics as a research language and computational laboratory.

  • Papers, preprints, conjectures and open problems
  • Python, SymPy, NumPy, Jupyter and other research tools
  • Literature review, modelling and reproducible experiments
LEVEL 06 · PROFESSIONAL & APPLIED

গণিত প্রয়োগ করুন

Connect mathematical methods to real operational and industry problems.

  • Finance, risk, AI/ML, engineering and cryptography
  • Case studies, modelling and simulation
  • Decision-focused quantitative analysis

STANDARD TOPIC MODEL

প্রতিটি topic শুধু “formula কী?”-এর উত্তর দেবে না

1 · ConceptPlain-language intuition and formal definition.
2 · PrerequisitesWhat the learner should know first.
3 · Formula & DerivationFormula, variables, conditions and derivation.
4 · Theorem & ProofRigorous statement, proof and counterexample where appropriate.
5 · Worked ExamplesSimple → intermediate → advanced applications.
6 · PracticeHints, solutions, difficulty and verification.
7 · ComputationVisualisation, symbolic or numerical experimentation.
8 · ApplicationReal-world use, limitations and assumptions.
