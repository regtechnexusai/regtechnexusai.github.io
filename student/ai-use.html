Student AI Use & Academic Integrity

AI should support learning, reasoning and revision—not replace a student's own understanding or violate course rules.

Good learning uses

  • Ask for explanations, examples and hints.
  • Generate practice questions and flashcards.
  • Ask AI to critique your reasoning after you attempt a problem.
  • Use AI to help organise research, then verify sources independently.

Use caution

  • Do not submit unreviewed AI output as your own work.
  • Follow your school, university, employer or professional body's AI policy.
  • Verify legal, regulatory, financial and accounting claims against authoritative sources.
  • Do not upload confidential or unnecessary personal information.

Source verification

AI can make mistakes. Important claims should be checked against primary or authoritative sources.
