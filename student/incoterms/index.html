A structured learning and transaction-review toolkit for students, trade professionals and finance learners. Move from the rule itself to obligations, delivery, risk transfer, carriage, insurance, customs, documentation and evidence.

The toolkit follows the Student Knowledge Hub learning model rather than treating Incoterms as a memorisation exercise.

1. Learn

Understand obligations, delivery, risk, carriage, insurance, clearance, costs and notices.

2. Practice

Compare terms, identify transport compatibility and map where risk and cost move.

3. Review

Use the transaction checklist to test the contract, named place, documents and responsibilities.

Professional Review Areas

These are review prompts, not legal or customs advice.

Contract

  • Rule and edition stated
  • Named place/port precise
  • Delivery point understood
  • Risk-transfer point understood

Trade Finance

  • Payment terms separately identified
  • LC/documentary requirements aligned
  • Transport document requirements checked
  • Insurance evidence aligned

Compliance

  • Export/import clearance responsibilities checked
  • Customs requirements reviewed
  • Invoice and shipment data reconciled
  • Applicable local requirements verified
