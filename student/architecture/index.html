2. International Standards & Country Code Navigator

Use these as learning gateways. A global standard or model code does not replace the law adopted by the project jurisdiction. Always identify the applicable country, state/province, city and authority before treating a requirement as project-applicable.

ISO 19650 — BIM information management

Learn how information is organized, exchanged, recorded and managed across the asset lifecycle.

ISO 21542 — Accessibility

A global reference for accessibility and usability of the built environment, including access, circulation and egress.

Open ISO 21542:2021 ↗
ICC I-Codes / IBC

A major international model-code family covering building safety topics. Treat it as a model-code reference and check local adoption/amendments.

Open ICC I-Codes ↗ · Digital Codes ↗
NFPA 101 — Life Safety

Use for study of life-safety concepts, movement of people and fire-related building protection. Verify the edition and local adoption.

3. Global Architecture Case-Study Lab

Do not only read the architect's name. Study the brief, context, circulation, material logic, structure, climate response and evidence. Then write what you would change if the project were placed in another context.

JAPAN · TADAO ANDO

Church of the Light / spatial sequence

Study how light, concrete, openings, boundaries and movement are used as architectural drivers. The Pritzker profile also documents Ando's approach to light, wind, water and spatial enclosure.

Study Pritzker profile ↗
INDIA · BALKRISHNA DOSHI

Aranya Low Cost Housing

Study housing, courtyards, pathways, social space, climate response and affordability. The Pritzker record describes how the project accommodates a large community through a system of houses, courtyards and internal paths.

Study Doshi case ↗
UNITED KINGDOM · ZAHA HADID

Form, representation & spatial experimentation

Use the Pritzker profile to study how spatial ideas, representation and unconventional forms can expand architectural possibilities. Compare concept sketches with built outcomes.

Study Zaha profile ↗
CHINA · WANG SHU

Context, material and regional identity

Use the Pritzker laureate archive to investigate how contemporary architecture can engage with local culture, material and place rather than treating context as decoration.

Study Wang Shu profile ↗
Case-study method: ① identify the brief → ② map site/context → ③ trace circulation → ④ mark structure/materials → ⑤ identify environmental strategy → ⑥ compare drawings/photos → ⑦ cite the source → ⑧ write your own design lesson.
