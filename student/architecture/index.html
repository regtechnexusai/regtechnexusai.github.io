International Standards & Regulatory Literacy

The goal is not to claim that one international standard replaces local law. Students should learn to map international frameworks to project requirements and then to the applicable jurisdictional code or regulation.

ISO & technical standards
Understand what a standard addresses, its scope and how it is applied.
Building & life safety
Study code concepts, fire/life safety and occupancy requirements.
Accessibility
Universal design, accessible circulation and inclusive environments.
Energy & environment
Energy performance, climate response and environmental design.
BIM & information
Model coordination, information requirements and structured digital workflows.
Sustainability frameworks
Performance-based thinking, resource efficiency and resilience.
Local building code
Translate international knowledge into the applicable national/local requirements.
Professional practice
Ethics, responsibility, documentation and interdisciplinary accountability.
