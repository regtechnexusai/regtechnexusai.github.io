Source-Verified Learning Briefs
Short original summaries built from official learning sources. Use these as starting points, then open the source for the full material. This section does not copy textbook or website content.




OpenAI
Study with active guidance
ChatGPT Study Mode is designed to guide students step by step, ask questions, give hints, check understanding and work with uploaded study materials. Treat AI output as study support and verify important information.
Source: OpenAI Study Mode documentation.
Read official source →


Google
AI + study materials
Google describes Gemini learning workflows that can help students study, create quizzes and flashcards, and work with class materials. Google also warns that generated content can contain mistakes and should be checked.
Source: Google for Education.
Read official source →


Perplexity
Research-oriented AI use
Perplexity's Education Pro documentation describes student-focused access including Learn Mode, search, file/image uploads and education-specific guidance. Use AI research tools to locate evidence, then inspect the original sources.
Source: Perplexity Help Center.
Read official source →


OpenStax
Free open textbooks
OpenStax provides free, openly licensed textbooks. Its digital books can be read online and many are available as free PDF downloads, making them useful starting points for structured subject study.
Source: OpenStax Higher Education.
Browse OpenStax →


MIT OCW
Course-level learning resources
MIT OpenCourseWare publishes free material from thousands of MIT courses, including syllabi, lecture notes, assignments and exams. Learners can study without enrollment and use the material at their own pace.
Source: MIT OpenCourseWare.
Browse MIT OCW →


Khan Academy
Practice and mastery
Khan Academy describes its student learning platform as free and built around practice and mastery across subjects. Use it alongside the Hub's notes and quizzes for additional practice.
Source: Khan Academy.
Open Khan Academy →
