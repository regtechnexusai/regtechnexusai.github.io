Foundation

Money Laundering

The process of disguising or transforming proceeds linked to criminal activity so their illicit origin is obscured. Study the concepts of placement, layering and integration as learning models, while recognising that legal definitions vary by jurisdiction.

Terrorist Financing

Focuses on the financing of terrorism and can involve funds from legitimate or illegitimate sources. The control objective is to identify and mitigate relevant financial risks.

Risk-Based Approach

AML/CFT controls should reflect identified ML/TF risks. FATF describes the risk-based approach as central to its standards and expects risks to be identified, assessed and understood.

Core Control Concepts

KYC / CDD

Understand customer identification, verification, purpose and intended nature of relationships, ongoing due diligence and risk-sensitive monitoring.

Beneficial Ownership

Learn why identifying the natural person(s) who ultimately own or control a legal person or arrangement matters for transparency and financial-crime risk.

Transaction Monitoring

Learn how institutions use rules, scenarios, analytics and investigation processes to identify potentially unusual activity. A red flag is an indicator for review, not proof of crime.

STR/SAR

Understand the concept of suspicious transaction/activity reporting and why escalation, investigation and reporting requirements depend on applicable law and institutional procedures.

Sanctions

Study sanctions screening as a financial-crime control and distinguish screening alerts from confirmed matches and legal conclusions.

Control Testing

Ask: What is the control objective? What evidence demonstrates operation? What exception was found? What remediation is required?

Quick Self-Test

1. What is the purpose of a risk-based approach?
To identify, assess and understand risks and apply proportionate measures to mitigate them.
2. Does a transaction-monitoring red flag prove money laundering?
No. It is an indicator that may require review; investigation and applicable legal processes determine next steps.
3. Why does beneficial ownership matter?
It improves transparency about the natural persons who ultimately own or control an entity or arrangement.

Primary references: FATF Recommendations and Bangladesh Financial Intelligence Unit (BFIU).
