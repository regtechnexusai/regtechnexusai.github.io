One consistent learning cycle

1 · Learn

Understand the script and rule through Bengali explanations and examples.

2 · Listen & repeat

Use reliable audio references; repeat short units and compare sound distinctions.

3 · Read & write

Recognise the native script, practise stroke order and produce words and sentences.

4 · Practise & review

Use recall prompts, mini quizzes, short writing tasks and spaced revision.

Teaching principle: Bengali-script pronunciation hints can support a first encounter but are not a substitute for native-script literacy or audio. Romanisation is a bridge, not the final learning goal. Listening and speaking exercises should use reliable audio; do not treat text alone as listening practice.

Course design and quality standards

  • Separate Bengali explanations from target-language examples.
  • Include script, pronunciation, spelling, vocabulary, grammar, reading, writing, listening and speaking in every pathway.
  • Use reliable dictionaries, official learning resources and clearly attributed audio sources.
  • Label lesson level and review date; verify official exam alignment before claiming a lesson maps to a current test syllabus.
  • Keep core lessons free and usable on mobile screens.
