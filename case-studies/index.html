Publication and quality controls

Publication policy: Automated monitoring may discover reports and prepare candidate records, but it will not publish AI-generated case analysis automatically. Each case must pass source verification, duplicate checks, legal-reference review, privacy/copyright checks and human approval. A red flag is not proof of wrongdoing, and the platform does not issue official regulatory determinations.
