Subject: Strengthening Monitoring of High-Value Cash Transactions
Date: 5 November 2026
Issuer: Head of Compliance, ABC Commercial Bank (Fictional)

Instructions:
1. All branches must review cash transactions exceeding BDT 10 lakh, subject to applicable regulatory reporting requirements and internal risk policies.
2. Branch Operations must ensure that customer identification and transaction purpose are properly documented.
3. Relationship Managers must review unusual transaction patterns and escalate suspicious activity to the Compliance Department.
4. The AML Compliance Unit must review escalated cases and document its assessment.
5. Branch Managers must submit a monthly compliance confirmation to the Head Office.
6. Any suspected suspicious transaction must be escalated promptly under the bank's applicable AML/CFT procedures. Internal escalation must not delay any legally required external reporting.

Implementation deadline: 30 November 2026.
Reporting frequency: Monthly.
