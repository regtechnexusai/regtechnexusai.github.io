PLAIN-LANGUAGE GUIDE

The key terms, in one minute.

These short definitions help first-time users read the source matrix. They are orientation only; the linked primary sources remain the authority for scope and interpretation.

TBML

Trade-based money laundering

Moving or disguising value through trade by manipulating price, quantity, description, documents or counterparties.

PF

Proliferation financing

Funding the development, acquisition or transfer of weapons of mass destruction or related delivery systems.

CDD

Customer due diligence

Identifying and verifying the customer and beneficial owner, understanding the relationship and applying ongoing review where required.

FEAT

Responsible-AI principles

Fairness, Ethics, Accountability and Transparency—the four principles used in the MAS responsible-AI reference path.

COSMIC

Financial-crime information sharing

Singapore’s secure financial-crime information-sharing platform and related public reference materials; this tool is not a replacement for it.

Scope note: This page uses regulator and law names only to identify public source paths. It is not produced, approved, endorsed, authorised or certified by any named authority.

SIGNAL DECISION LOGIC

Critical controls override the aggregate indicator.

The coverage indicator is secondary. The primary result is a source-derived control gate, with a separate lane view so a strong area cannot hide a material gap elsewhere.

BLOCKEDA critical control is Does not meet or Not assessed. Escalate and remediate before relying on the process.
CONDITIONALA critical control is Partial, or the route is incomplete. Assign an owner and target date.
REMEDIATEA non-critical control is open or partially evidenced. Review the control design and add evidence.
REVIEWABLEAll applicable critical controls are evidenced and no open control remains. This is not certification.

PUBLIC / INTERNAL BOUNDARY

Useful in public. Governed in private.

This page is for early-stage review, training, workshop preparation and evidence-gap discussion. A bank or regulated institution would need its own approved methodology, current source validation, authorised access, secure evidence handling, audit trail and qualified human review before relying on an output.
