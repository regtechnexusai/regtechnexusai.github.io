Use this safely

Do not enter customer names, account numbers, transaction details, personal data or confidential documents. This is an independent browser-based review aid. It is not produced, approved, endorsed, authorised or certified by MAS, VARA, DFSA, CBUAE, AUSTRAC, APRA, ASIC or any government authority.

HOW IT WORKS

A result you can use in the next meeting.

Each answer produces a visible result. Nothing is hidden behind an unexplained percentage.

01

Choose the perimeter

Start with Singapore, Dubai/UAE or Australia. Dubai is kept jurisdiction-aware rather than treated as one generic UAE rulebook.

02

Rate the evidence

For each control, select Meets, Partial, Does not meet, Not assessed or Not applicable. Use a non-sensitive project label only.

03

Assess the matrix

Press Assess this matrix only after completing every control. The source row, result, risk level and next action then appear together.

04

Use the action plan

Take the critical gaps and next actions into your internal review. PDF/CSV export is intentionally reserved for a later validated version.

RISK DECISION LOGIC

The tool does not average away a critical gap.

The evidence-readiness percentage is secondary. The primary result is the control-level risk classification shown in the source-to-control matrix.

CriticalCritical control is Does not meet or Not assessed. Escalate and remediate before relying on the process.
HighCritical control is Partial, or a non-critical control Does not meet. Assign an owner and target date.
MediumNon-critical control is Partial or Not assessed. Add evidence and review the control design.
ControlledEvidence is reported as meeting the control. Validate source version and evidence quality; this is not certification.
INTERACTIVE TOOL

Build your readiness snapshot.

Complete the controls that apply to your use case. A control marked Missing or Not assessed remains an open action; a Partial answer counts as half-complete until evidence is strengthened.

PUBLIC / INTERNAL BOUNDARY

Useful in public. Governed in private.

This page is for early-stage review, training, workshop preparation and evidence-gap discussion. A bank or regulated institution would need its own approved methodology, current source validation, authorised access, secure evidence handling, audit trail and qualified human review before relying on an output.

Public tool can doStructure questions, calculate an indicative evidence-readiness score, highlight open gaps and create a short action summary.
Public tool cannot doMake a legal conclusion, file a report, replace a regulator platform, screen live customers or certify compliance.
Keep internalCustomer data, transaction evidence, case files, regulator correspondence, model-validation records and board decisions.
