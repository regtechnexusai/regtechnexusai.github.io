Privacy at RegTech Nexus AI

Effective date: 10 November 2026

This notice describes the current public website and GaaS preview at a high level. It is not a substitute for a tailored privacy notice, data-processing agreement or legal advice. Product and provider configurations may change; material changes should be reflected here before production release.

Information we receive

We may receive information you choose to send by email or through a future enquiry workflow, such as your name, organisation, contact details and message. We use it to respond to your request, maintain the website and improve our services.

Technical information and analytics

Hosting, delivery and security providers may process technical information such as IP address, browser and device information, request time and requested URL for service delivery, security and reliability. Google Analytics is loaded on this website (measurement ID G-TF41MZMKPN) and may process usage and device information, including identifiers or cookies, under Google's applicable terms and settings. Do not assume analytics is disabled or consent-managed unless the live site provides and enforces an appropriate consent control.

Security challenge

The GaaS research workflow uses Cloudflare Turnstile. When the challenge is loaded or completed, Cloudflare may process technical and interaction information, including IP address and browser signals, to assess automated abuse. Cloudflare's own privacy terms also apply to that service.

Browser-based tools

The public calculators and toolkits are browser-based review aids. The public build does not provide a server-side institutional case database, login, role-based access control or a customer-facing case audit trail. Do not enter confidential customer, transaction, employee or supervisory information. Email buttons open your own email client; they do not submit a form to this website.

GaaS live research preview

When you explicitly run live research in the GaaS preview, the browser sends the structured fields you entered (which may include jurisdiction, topic, authority, institution or business context, process and research question) and a Cloudflare Turnstile token to the GaaS staging research API hosted on Cloudflare Workers. The request is sent over HTTPS. The preview does not upload or process selected supporting documents; document processing is not enabled. Do not submit personal data, customer records, account numbers, transaction data, SAR/STR information, confidential institutional material, credentials or other restricted information.

The staging API may process submitted research text to provide an AI-assisted response and may use external research or AI service providers configured on the backend. The exact provider chain, processing locations, retention periods, logging configuration and deletion arrangements have not been independently verified for this notice and must not be assumed. Do not use the staging preview for production, regulated or confidential workloads. No account or persistent customer workspace is offered through this public preview.

GaaS outputs are research candidates, not verified legal conclusions. A human professional must verify original sources, current status, applicability and effective dates.

Retention and sharing

We do not sell personal information. Enquiry information may be retained for as long as reasonably needed to respond, maintain business records and meet applicable obligations. Technical logs, analytics data, Turnstile signals and GaaS research requests may be subject to the retention and deletion practices of the relevant hosting, analytics, security and backend service providers. Exact GaaS API and downstream provider retention periods have not yet been confirmed; do not submit information that requires a guaranteed deletion period or restricted handling.

Service providers may process information on our behalf to host, secure, analyse or operate the website and preview. Their own terms and privacy notices may also apply.

International processing

Cloud hosting, analytics, security and AI/research services may process information in countries outside Bangladesh. The precise locations and transfer arrangements depend on provider configuration and have not been verified for every GaaS staging request.

External links

This website links to external services, including professional, social and hosted application platforms. Their own privacy policies apply when you leave this website.
