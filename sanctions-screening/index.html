How the screening decision is structured

01 · NormalizePreserve original values while applying documented candidate-generation rules.
02 · Candidate matchExact, token and edit-distance signals identify records for review.
03 · CorroborateDOB, nationality, address and identifiers strengthen or weaken a candidate.
04 · ReviewA human reviewer determines the final disposition; algorithmic similarity is not a legal conclusion.

Authoritative source families

  • UN Security Council Consolidated List — official UN source.
  • OFAC Sanctions List Service — official U.S. Treasury source.
  • EU sanctions resources — European Commission source family.
  • UK Sanctions List — current UK source for sanctions designations.

Source availability is not equivalent to screening clearance. A failed or stale source load must produce an incomplete-screening state, never a false “no match”.
