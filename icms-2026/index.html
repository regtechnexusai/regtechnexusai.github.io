CONTROL ARCHITECTURE

Accountability has
three lines.

The checklist uses the Three Lines of Defense as a practical conversation structure. It does not assess a bank’s compliance on behalf of the regulator.

FIRST LINE

Business units

Own the risks in day-to-day activities, execute controls, preserve evidence and escalate exceptions.

SECOND LINE

Compliance & risk

Set frameworks, monitor effectiveness, provide specialist challenge and coordinate corrective action.

THIRD LINE

Internal audit

Provide independent, objective assurance to the Board and Audit Committee on governance and controls.

METHODOLOGY & BOUNDARIES

Useful structure.
Professional judgment.

Scoring logic

Each prompt is scored from 0 to 3. The 24-question instrument has a maximum indicative score of 72. This composite score is an independently created RegTech Nexus AI diagnostic for internal prioritisation—not a Bangladesh Bank rating. The circular contains separate official scoring and risk-rating frameworks, including Chapters 3.5, 6.4 and 7.5 and Annexures A, B and G; see the framework references above.

Evidence discipline

Use the strongest available evidence: approved documents, meeting records, reports, system extracts, testing results, issue registers and closure validation. A “3” should be supported by evidence, not intention.

Data boundary

This static page processes assessment answers in the browser. Do not enter customer names, account data, transaction details, confidential documents or regulated personal information.
