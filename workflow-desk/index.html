Connected Workflow Desk

A browser-local coordination workspace to organise regulatory-change reviews, policy/control observations, ICMS actions and trade-finance review hand-offs in one portable case record.

Prototype boundary: This first release coordinates reviewer-entered information and exports/imports a portable JSON case file. It does not automatically connect to the tools, verify regulatory sources, authenticate evidence, run legal analysis, calculate risk scores or store cases on a server. Use synthetic or public, non-sensitive information only. Do not enter customer, KYC, transaction, SAR/STR or confidential bank-policy data.

5 · Review and assurance gates

Gate 1 Source authority, status, version and dates independently verified.

Gate 2 Applicability and affected entities/processes confirmed by Compliance/Legal.

Gate 3 Original document references and evidence independently checked.

Gate 4 Control owner validates design and operating evidence.

Gate 5 Decisions, approvals, remediation and retesting are recorded.

Important: A tool-generated signal is not proof of a control failure, legal obligation or compliance status. Human review remains mandatory.
