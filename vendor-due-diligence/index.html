2. Request institutional access

To request a VDD discussion or dossier review, send a written request from your institutional email address. Requests are reviewed manually. A business email domain is preferred; additional verification may be required where the sender's authority or institutional affiliation cannot be established from the request alone.

Do not attach confidential documents. Do not send customer records, account numbers, transaction data, credentials, supervisory-confidential information or internal security findings in the initial request. No file upload is provided on this static page.

Include the following in your email:

  • Institution name and official website domain.
  • Your name, role and institutional email address.
  • Service under evaluation: Policy Gap Analyzer, TradeGuard, ICMS Toolkit or general vendor review.
  • Purpose and expected scope of the assessment.
  • Whether an NDA or institutional authorisation process applies.

3. Review and disclosure process

  1. Request received: the institution submits a written request with non-confidential identifying details.
  2. Verification: institutional affiliation, requester authority and evaluation purpose are reviewed.
  3. Scope confirmation: relevant services, requested documents, confidentiality requirements and availability are confirmed.
  4. Controlled disclosure: approved materials may be shared through a separately selected channel appropriate to the sensitivity and agreed terms.
  5. Follow-up: questions, clarifications and any agreed commitments are documented in writing.

Do not assume that an emailed request creates a service contract, establishes an SLA, or guarantees dossier access. No password-protected dashboard or automated identity-verification system is provided by this static page.

4. Service boundaries

VDD responses are scoped to the service being evaluated and to evidence actually available. A public website review or document pack does not replace the institution's own TPRM assessment, security testing, legal review, outsourcing assessment or regulatory notification obligations.
