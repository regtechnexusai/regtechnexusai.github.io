METHOD & GOVERNANCE

Exceptional means
inspectable.

The product is intentionally designed around evidence, source traceability and accountable human review. The launch claim is narrow enough to be trusted and useful enough to be used.

01 · Source registry

Each pack identifies its jurisdiction, source path, review date and intended use. Official pages are linked; protected text is not reproduced.

02 · Hybrid roadmap

The public MVP uses transparent browser logic. A future private version may add retrieval and AI only with version pinning, validation and audit controls.

03 · No false precision

The tool reports review classifications and evidence gaps. It does not create an invented percentage of legal compliance.

04 · Human decision owner

Policy approval, customer action, filing, escalation and regulatory interpretation remain with the authorised institution and its professionals.
