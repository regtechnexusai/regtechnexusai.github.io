WHAT WE DO

Turning complexity
into clarity.

Regulation, risk and technological change are increasingly interconnected. RegTech Nexus AI develops structured intelligence and practical tools for professionals operating in regulated environments.

Our work is governance-led, evidence-aware and designed to support—not replace—responsible human judgment.

PRACTITIONER-LED

Entrepreneurship
backed by expert insight.

RegTech Nexus AI was created by Rezwana AKHI, a woman entrepreneur and owner of Travel To Know (traveltoknow.com.bd), an IATA-accredited travel agency.

For RegTech Nexus AI, she has established an expert advisory board comprising former central-banking officials, bank and finance-company officials, technology specialists, and professionals with experience in Risk-Based Supervision (RBS), FIU operations, banking regulation and financial intelligence.

The platform brings together entrepreneurship, technology and specialist regulatory insight to develop independent research, practical frameworks, explainers and digital solutions for regulated institutions and professionals.

RegTech Nexus AI is an independent knowledge and solutions platform. Its materials do not constitute official instructions, directives or regulatory guidance from any central bank or Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU).
