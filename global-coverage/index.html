HOW COVERAGE IS VERIFIED

From global standard to country-level evidence

A source directory is only the starting point. Each country/topic assessment must be supported by its own official legal and supervisory evidence.

  1. Identify the baselineRecord the international standard, version, issuing body and instrument type.
  2. Locate domestic authorityIdentify the national regulator, FIU, central bank, legislature or competent authority.
  3. Verify the local instrumentConfirm official text, scope, effective date, amendments and supersession.
  4. Assess implementationDistinguish adoption from operational effectiveness, supervision and enforcement.
  5. Record the gapDocument evidence, confidence, limitation, owner and next review date.

Important scope and reliance notice

This initial portal is a directory of selected international source bodies and review domains. It is not yet a complete country-by-country regulatory database, does not certify legal compliance and must not be used as the sole basis for a regulatory decision. Source links and time-sensitive status must be rechecked before operational use. National laws, regulator rules, effective dates and supersession must be verified separately.

For a country-specific review or correction, contact RegTech Nexus AI.
