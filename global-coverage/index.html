MASTER BLUEPRINT

How a coverage record becomes auditable

Every jurisdiction/topic record should be evidence-led, versioned and clear about what has—and has not—been verified.

  1. Define scopeResolve jurisdiction names, aliases, territories and regional frameworks.
  2. Identify baselineRecord source body, instrument type, version/date and official URL.
  3. Verify domestic lawCapture national instrument, regulator, legal status, effective date and amendments.
  4. Assess implementationSeparate adoption, effectiveness, supervisory practice and enforcement evidence.
  5. Log the gapAssign status, priority, evidence limitation, next action and review date.

Coverage status definitions

Not assessed

No structured jurisdiction/topic assessment is recorded.

Partially verified

Some official evidence has been reviewed, but material gaps remain.

Verified as of date

Official text, amendments and supersession were checked on a recorded date.

Verification blocked

An official source was identified but could not be reviewed; this is not a “no law” finding.
