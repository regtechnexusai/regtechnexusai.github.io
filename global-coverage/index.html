A REPEATABLE ANALYSIS METHOD

From signal to defensible action

The same evidence discipline can support many FinTech questions without forcing every problem into one scoring model.

  1. 01
    Frame the question

    Define the product, transaction, institution, jurisdiction and decision that need analysis.

  2. 02
    Verify authoritative sources

    Check the primary source, date, legal status, scope and amendments. International standards are not automatically domestic law.

  3. 03
    Select the dedicated tool

    Use the relevant existing module; do not treat one tool's output as another tool's result.

  4. 04
    Inspect evidence and limitations

    Record what is known, missing, assumed or not verified. Risk indicators are not proof of wrongdoing.

  5. 05
    Assign action and retest

    Where appropriate, record the owner, due date, supporting evidence, approval and retest result in the institution's approved process.
