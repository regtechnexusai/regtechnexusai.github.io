Intelligence Beyond Rules. A regulatory change analysis workspace to compare document versions, surface potential obligation signals and organise a human-reviewed impact assessment.

Early prototype · Rule-based · Human review required
Important limitation: This prototype runs in your browser. It does not verify regulatory sources, provide legal advice, make compliance determinations or use a live AI model. Use public documents or synthetic examples only. Do not enter confidential banking, customer, KYC or transaction data.
