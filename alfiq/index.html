Connected regulatory workflow

AlfiQ is the regulatory-change triage workspace. Use the existing RegTech Nexus AI tools for their distinct review stages; open each tool separately and transfer only non-sensitive, reviewer-approved information manually.

1. Verify the regulatory source

Check the issuing authority, instrument status, publication and effective dates, and source references before relying on a change signal.

Open Regulatory Source Register

2. Compare and triage the change

Use AlfiQ to compare text versions, record potential signals and organise human review. Its current prototype does not authenticate sources or determine legal applicability.

Use AlfiQ workspace

3. Map policy and control gaps

Use the browser-local Policy Gap Analyzer to identify policy-to-control review signals and prepare an evidence-gap action plan.

Open Policy Gap Analyzer

4. Assess governance and remediation

Use ICMS for broader internal-control governance, ownership, evidence review and corrective-action follow-up. This is a separate toolkit.

Open ICMS Toolkit

5. Explain, challenge and review

Use the Explainable AI and Agentic AI Governance guidance to structure rationale, human challenge, approval gates and monitoring expectations.

Explainable AI · Agentic AI Governance

6. Check tool scope and trust disclosures

Review each tool’s stated capabilities, limitations, evidence expectations and responsible-use disclosures before institutional use.
