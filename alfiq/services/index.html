Important operating boundaries

  • AlfiQ is currently an early-stage, browser-based decision-support prototype with rule-based and similarity-assisted text analysis; it is not a production AI SaaS platform.
  • Similarity-based matches are candidates, not proof of legal or semantic equivalence.
  • Source authenticity, legal status, applicability and effective dates must be independently verified against the original regulator publication.
  • Suggested owners and evidence are triage prompts, not confirmed control gaps or assigned responsibilities.
  • The service is not legal advice, regulatory approval, a compliance certification or an autonomous compliance determination.
  • Initial pilots are limited to public regulatory documents or non-confidential materials the customer is authorised to share. Do not send personal data, KYC records, transaction records, suspicious activity reports, confidential bank policies or customer-identifying material.
  • Secure multi-tenant document storage, customer accounts, continuous monitoring, payment processing and automated subscriptions are not currently provided.
Before engagement: A written scope, fee, delivery schedule, permitted document use, confidentiality arrangements and limitations statement will be agreed. Customer materials will not be submitted to third-party AI services as part of this initial prototype-led pilot.
